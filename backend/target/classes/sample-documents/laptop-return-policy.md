# Laptop Return Policy

Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Category: Policies
Region: GLOBAL

## General Return Rules
Customers may return products purchased on ShopSmart within 30 days of delivery. All returned items must include original packaging and accessories.

## Opened Products
Opened laptops can be returned within 7 days if physical damage is not present. Devices must be reset to factory settings with all account locks removed before shipping back.

## Damaged Items
Products that arrive damaged must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. A replacement or full refund will be issued upon carrier verification.
